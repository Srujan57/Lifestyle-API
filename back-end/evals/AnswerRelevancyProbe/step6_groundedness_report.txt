  QUESTION   : What lifestyle changes can I make to protect my heart while being treated for prostate cancer?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BOT ANSWER:
It's great that you're looking to protect your heart health during your prostate cancer
treatment! Here are some lifestyle changes that can help:

1. **Stay Active**: Regular physical activity can reduce fatigue, improve mood, and help
maintain muscle mass. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise each week,
like walking or gentle strength training. Always listen to your body and adjust based on how you
feel.

2. **Eat a Heart-Healthy Diet**: Focus on whole foods, including plenty of fruits, vegetables,
whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit saturated fats, added sugars, and sodium. Foods rich in
omega-3 fatty acids (like fish, walnuts, and flaxseeds) can be particularly beneficial for heart
health.

3. **Manage Stress**: Stress can impact your heart health. Consider incorporating relaxation
techniques such as deep breathing, meditation, or yoga into your routine.

4. **Get Enough Sleep**: Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep each night. Good sleep is essential
for overall health and recovery.

5. **Monitor Your Health**: Keep an eye on your blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar
levels, as cancer treatments can increase the risk of cardiovascular issues.

6. **Avoid Tobacco**: If you smoke, seek support to quit. Smoking significantly increases heart
disease risk.

7. **Stay Hydrated**: Drink plenty of water, especially if you're experiencing side effects from
treatment.

These changes can help improve your heart health and overall well-being. If you have specific
concerns or symptoms, always consult your healthcare team for personalized advice.
